Pipeline Architecture

Data Cleansing & Filtering

B
OneHotEncoder (Categoricals)

Categorical attributes (model, transmission, fuelType) expanded with out-of-vocabulary fallback handling.

C
StandardScaler (Numericals)

Standardized zero-mean unit-variance scaling on year, mileage, tax, mpg, and engineSize.
